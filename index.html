Privacy Policy – Radha Naam Counter

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Radha Naam Counter ("the app") helps you count naam jap by listening to your chanting. It is built to work entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. Microphone and your voice

  • The app uses the microphone only while you have started listening. A notification is shown the whole time the microphone is in use, and you can pause or stop it at any time.
  • With the default Offline Hindi engine, your voice is processed on your phone only. No audio and no transcripts are sent anywhere.
  • No audio is ever recorded or saved. Recognised words are used only to update your counts and are not stored.
  • If you choose the optional "Android speech service" engine in Settings, your phone's speech service (usually provided by Google) performs the recognition and may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send audio anywhere.

2. "Teach my voice"

If you use My voice mode, the app stores a mathematical sound profile ("voice graph") of the short samples you record, so that it can compare your chanting with them. These profiles are not audio recordings, are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, and are deleted when you delete the samples, the naam, or the app.

3. Data stored on your phone

Your naam list, counts, history, settings and chosen background photo are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The background photo is a private copy you choose through Android's photo picker; the app cannot see your other photos. You can export your history as a CSV file yourself; the app never uploads it.

4. Donations ("Support the founder")

Optional donations are processed entirely by Google Play. The app does not see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to these payments.

5. Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no account or sign-in. The app's internet permission is there only because Google Play's billing component needs it for optional donations; the app sends no data of its own over the internet.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes all of its data from your phone. Dashboard → Reset deletes all counts and history.
